Frag: New Heights

Academic Project | September 2025 — October 2025

In a team of 2, I worked alongside my partner to design and develop an expansion pack for the pre-existing game Frag by Steve Jackson Games.

My main responsibility during this project was to take our ideas and translate them into Frag's signature style. I did this by using the existing cards as a base and creating new ones in Clip Studio Paint PRO, as well as translating the new mechanics into understandable game instructions based on the terminology and writing style of the original game. In addition, I took careful and detailed notes during in-class playtesting.

The expansion, alongside other groups in the class, has been recognized by Steve Jackson Games on their official blog as of December 27th, 2025. You can view the blog here.
